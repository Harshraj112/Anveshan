Case brief

On 14 Aug 2026, confidential vendor rate files left the Pune branch of a logistics company. The suspect, R. Mehra, says he was at a client site that afternoon and shared a screenshot showing a refund he received.

    Hypotheses

    Which story fits the evidence

    Next evidence to collect: router DHCP logs for 12 Aug. They would show if the unknown login came from inside the office, which separates story A from story B.

    The three stories

    • A. Insider leak for money
      Mehra copied the files and sold them to an outside buyer.
    • B. Account was hacked
      Someone else used Mehra's login to steal the files.
    • C. Normal work, misread
      The copy was allowed and the payment is a real refund.

    The assistant can only answer from the evidence. Every sentence carries the ID and hash of the item it came from. A sentence without support is struck out, and every answer is saved in the custody log.
